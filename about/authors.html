Ari

Ari (they/them) is a non-binary trans person from the United States, currently completing the final year of their PhD. Their professional research focuses on the molecular biology of HIV, although they come from a training background in reproductive biology and synthetic biology. Unprofessionally, they have an interest in DIY harm reduction and creating fun and accessible resources to educate other trans people about the biology of transition. Outside of research they enjoy video games, singing in a gay choir, platform shoes, and clowncore fashion.

esmeralda

esmeralda (she/they/it) is a non-binary trans woman. Since beginning its gender transition by DIY methods, it has closely followed the literature on the biology and social aspects of this process and made efforts to share knowledge with its local community and promote trans welfare in its professional circles. They are described by themself as a “failed mathematician” — owing to their doctorate work in algebraic geometry — and by others as both a “very famous researcher in probabilistic modelling” and a “very dangerous woman”. She lives in Scotland, but can often be found traveling the world, acquainting herself with queer communities through their language, music, and shared experiences.

Emily

Emily (she/her) is a trans woman living in the bay area working in organic chemistry and analytical chemistry. In 2025, she initially researched and wrote about nonstandard transition topics and in mid 2026 self-published a paper detailing an alternative synthesis method for the estradiol prodrug EC508. She currently runs Artemis Analytical, a harm reduction lab using HPLC and FTIR to verify the identity and concentration of DIY HRT.

  • A Proposed Synthesis and Characterization Protocol for EC508, a Sulfonamide–Proline Estradiol Prodrug (Emily; 2026)
