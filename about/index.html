About

WHSAH is a global collective of trans people dedicated to furthering the science and healthcare relevant to trans people. In addition to this, we connect people doing liberatory harm reduction across the world, and give a voice to trans people and the DIY community in the diverse political and societal discourse surrounding the hard sciences. Indirectly, our work also benefits other groups which are commonly discriminated against and marginalized in society and the healthcare system.

WHSAH?

Yes, it’s an acronym.

How do you pronounce that?

There is no right or wrong way to pronounce WHSAH. In no particular order, here’s some suggestions by our authors:
[ˈväˌsä], [vsä], [wʌˈsɑ], [ˈwʊ.sɐ], [ˈwäˌsä], or even [wəˈhɑ].

Funding notice

WHSAH is funded entirely by contributions of its members and donations of trans people. As a non-profit, all of us are volunteers; the funding is used to pay for administrative efforts, website and hosting costs. This is one step to ensuring that our incentives are aligned with the needs of trans people.

If you’d like to contribute financially, please reach out via email.

Imprint

This website is a publication of WHSAH, a non-profit association according to Art. 60 ff. of the Swiss Civil Code seated in Zürich. Its purpose is to promote interdisciplinary research and science pertaining to trans people, to further the inclusion and acceptance of trans people in society, and to participate in political and societal discourse promoting the interests and needs of trans people.
